🎈 Balloon Popping Game 🎈

How to Play

  • Allow camera access when prompted — the game uses hand tracking!
  • Use your index finger to pop the balloons on the screen.
  • Each popped balloon adds to your score.
  • You'll have 30 balloons — try to pop them all!
  • Once all are popped or rise off screen, the game ends.

Tips:

  • Keep your hand within the camera view.
  • Ensure good lighting for accurate hand detection.
  • Play in front of your webcam (desktop/laptop recommended).
